Rhodes Scholarship 2027 for International Students Fully-Funded


Introduction

The Rhodes Scholarship is one of the world's oldest and most prestigious international scholarship programs. Established in 1902 through the will of Cecil Rhodes, the scholarship provides exceptional students from around the world with the opportunity to pursue postgraduate studies at University of Oxford in the United Kingdom.

The scholarship is designed to identify and support outstanding young leaders who demonstrate academic excellence, leadership potential, integrity, and a commitment to making a positive impact in the world. Each year, Rhodes Scholars are selected from numerous countries and regions to join a diverse community of talented individuals studying a wide range of disciplines at Oxford.

In addition to covering the costs of study, the Rhodes Scholarship offers scholars access to a global network of influential alumni, leadership development opportunities, and a world-class educational experience. The program aims to nurture future leaders who will contribute meaningfully to their professions, communities, and society as a whole.

Why Choose Rhodes Scholarship?

The Rhodes Scholarship is widely regarded as one of the most prestigious scholarships in the world, offering students the opportunity to study at the University of Oxford, one of the world's leading universities. It provides scholars with access to exceptional academic resources, renowned faculty, and a rich intellectual environment that encourages innovation, critical thinking, and personal growth.

Beyond financial support, the Rhodes Scholarship focuses on developing future leaders who are committed to making a positive impact in society. Scholars become part of a diverse international community of talented individuals from different backgrounds and disciplines, creating lifelong friendships and professional connections. The scholarship also provides access to a global network of Rhodes alumni, many of whom have gone on to become influential leaders in government, business, science, education, and other fields.

For students who aspire to combine academic excellence with leadership and service, the Rhodes Scholarship offers a unique opportunity to gain a world-class education, expand their global perspective, and build a strong foundation for future success.

Who Can Apply for Rhodes Scholarship?

The Rhodes Scholarship is intended for exceptional students and young graduates who aspire to continue their education at the University of Oxford. It is open to applicants from countries and regions that participate in the Rhodes Scholarship program, giving talented individuals from different parts of the world the opportunity to pursue postgraduate studies in the United Kingdom.

The scholarship attracts a diverse range of applicants, including final-year university students, recent graduates, and young professionals who have demonstrated strong academic performance and a desire to contribute positively to society. Candidates come from various fields such as science, engineering, medicine, business, law, social sciences, and the humanities.

More than academic success, the Rhodes Scholarship seeks individuals who show initiative, leadership qualities, and a commitment to serving others. It is therefore an excellent opportunity for ambitious students who want to combine advanced education with personal growth, international exposure, and the chance to join a global community of future leaders.

Eligibility Criteria for Rhodes Scholarship

Eligibility Criteria
  • Must be from an eligible Rhodes constituency(eligible country) and apply through only one constituency. CLICK here to see if your country is eligible
  • If you have dual citizenship or ties to more than one country, you should apply through the place you are most strongly connected to.
  • Age rules depend on the constituency, but current examples include 18-23 for India and the United States, 18-24 for Australia, and 18-24 or 18-25 for West Africa depending on the course group.
  • Older applicants may still qualify in some constituencies if they are under 27 and finished their first undergraduate degree within the required recent time window.
  • You must have already completed, or be about to complete, an undergraduate degree before starting at Oxford.
  • You must meet the entry requirements of your chosen full-time postgraduate course at Oxford.
  • A very strong academic record is expected. Rhodes notes that candidates are more competitive for Oxford admission with a First Class Honours degree, a GPA of 3.70/4.0 or higher, or an equivalent result.
  • If English is not your first language, you must meet Oxford's English language standard for your course.
  • The scholarship is only for full-time study at the University of Oxford; part-time study is not covered.
  • PGCert and PGDip courses are not eligible.
  • You can submit only one application per year, and you can apply a maximum of two times in total.
  • Some constituencies have extra rules. For example, the U.S. constituency requires citizenship or lawful permanent residency, and the Australian constituency accepts citizens, permanent residents, and in some cases refugees or asylum seekers living in Australia.
  • West Africa has an additional residency rule: applicants must have lived in one or more eligible West African countries for at least five of the last ten years.
  • Selection is also based on Rhodes's core qualities: academic excellence, full use of talents, service to others, leadership, and strong moral character.

Benefits of Rhodes Scholarship

  • Full coverage of University of Oxford course fees.
  • Annual living stipend, paid to help with accommodation and everyday expenses.
  • Payment of the fee required to apply to Oxford.
  • Visa fee support, including the Student visa cost and the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS).
  • Two economy flights: one to the UK at the start of study and one back home at the end.
  • A settling-in allowance when scholars arrive in Oxford.
  • Extra visa-renewal and IHS support if a scholar moves into a second course in Oxford.
  • Access to Oxford's academic, college, and student life as a full-time postgraduate scholar.
  • Membership in the global Rhodes Scholar community and access to Rhodes House support and programming.
  • Financial and pastoral support from the Rhodes Trust throughout the scholarship period.
  • Small grants and emergency help may also be available for research, travel, and creative opportunities.

How to Apply for Rhodes Scholarship

Applications for the Rhodes Scholarship are submitted through the official Rhodes Trust application portal. Applicants must first select their eligible country or region (constituency) and then access the corresponding application page, where they can find all application requirements, deadlines, and submission details.
