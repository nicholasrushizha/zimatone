Romania Government Scholarship 2027 for International Students Fully-Funded


Introduction


The Romania Government Scholarship is a fully funded international scholarship program offered by the Romanian Government through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFA) for students from non-European Union countries. The scholarship allows international students to pursue bachelor's, master's, and PhD studies at accredited universities across Romania. The program aims to promote international education, cultural exchange, and academic cooperation by giving talented students access to high-quality higher education in Romania. Scholarship recipients receive financial support that covers major study expenses, making Romania an attractive destination for students seeking affordable and internationally recognized education.

Why Choose Romania Government Scholarship?


Romania has become an increasingly popular destination for international students due to its high-quality education, affordable cost of living, and internationally recognized degrees. As a member of the European Union, Romania offers students the opportunity to study in a European academic environment while benefiting from significantly lower living and tuition costs compared to many Western European countries. In addition to its strong universities, Romania provides a rich cultural experience, modern student facilities, and a safe and welcoming environment for international students. Through the Romania Government Scholarship, students can access quality education, build international networks, and gain valuable academic and professional opportunities while studying in the heart of Europe.

Who Can Apply for Romania Government Scholarship?

The Romania Government Scholarship is open to international students from non-European Union countries who wish to pursue higher education in Romania. The program welcomes students at different academic levels, including those seeking bachelor's, master's, and PhD degrees at accredited Romanian universities.

Applications are accepted from high school graduates, university graduates, final-year students awaiting graduation, and researchers interested in advanced academic studies. The scholarship is designed for students who are motivated to study in Romania and gain access to quality education while experiencing the country's rich culture and academic environment.

The program attracts applicants from a wide range of academic backgrounds and fields of study, making it an excellent opportunity for students looking to further their education in Europe through a fully funded scholarship supported by the Romanian Government.

Eligibility Criteria for Romania Government Scholarship

To be eligible for the Romania Government Scholarship, applicants must meet the following requirements:

Citizenship Requirements
  • Must be a citizen of a non-European Union (non-EU) country
  • Romanian citizens are not eligible
  • Citizens of Romanian origin or Romanian historical communities are not eligible under this program
  • Applicants with refugee or protected status in Romania are not eligible

Academic Requirements
  • Must have a minimum academic average of 7/10 (or equivalent “Good” grade) in the most recently completed studies
  • Academic documents must be issued by recognized and accredited institutions
  • Applicants must have completed the previous level of education required for their chosen program

Eligible Study Levels
  • Bachelor's Degree
  • Master's Degree
  • PhD Degree

Language Requirements
  • Bachelor's and Master's programs are generally taught in Romanian
  • A free Romanian language preparatory year is provided for students who do not know Romanian
  • PhD applicants may choose programs in Romanian, English, or other foreign languages depending on the university

Age Limit
  • There is no official age limit for the Romania Government Scholarship program. Applicants of any age can apply as long as they meet the academic and eligibility requirements.

Who Cannot Apply?
  • Citizens of EU member countries
  • Romanian citizens
  • Current beneficiaries of a Romanian state scholarship for the same study cycle
  • Members of diplomatic and consular missions accredited to Romania and their families
  • Staff members of international organizations based in Romania and their families
  • Stateless persons officially recognized in Romania

Fields Not Covered
The scholarship does not accept applications in:
  • Medicine
  • Dental Medicine
  • Pharmacy

Applicants may apply to most other academic fields offered by accredited Romanian universities.

Benefits of Romania Government Scholarship

The Romania Government Scholarship is a fully funded scholarship that provides international students with financial and academic support throughout their studies in Romania.

Scholarship Benefits
  • Full tuition fee waiver
  • Free accommodation in university dormitories (subject to availability)
  • Monthly scholarship stipend
  • Free Romanian language preparatory year (if required)
  • Access to accredited Romanian universities
  • Study in a European Union country
  • Internationally recognized degree
  • Access to university libraries, laboratories, and academic facilities
  • Opportunity to experience European culture and education
  • Support for bachelor's, master's, and PhD studies

Romania Government Scholarship remains one of the most attractive fully funded scholarship opportunities in Europe for students from non-EU countries.

Application Dates


  • Applications Open: 16 February 2027
  • Application Deadline: 31 March 2027
  • Late Applications: Not accepted

Applicants must submit their applications through the official Study in Romania scholarship portal before the deadline. All required documents must be uploaded and submitted by 31 March 2026.

How to Apply for Romania Government Scholarship

Applications for the Romania Government Scholarship are submitted online through the official Study in Romania Scholarship Platform managed by the Romanian Government. All application guidelines, eligibility requirements, required documents, and scholarship updates are available on the official portal below.

Romania Government Scholarship Website



Applicants should create an account on the platform, review the official application instructions, and submit their application before the stated deadline. The portal is the only official channel for applying to the Romania Government Scholarship.
