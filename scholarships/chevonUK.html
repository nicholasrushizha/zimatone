Chevening Scholarship 2027 for International Students Fully-Funded


Introduction


The Chevening Scholarship is a fully funded UK government international scholarship programme designed to support outstanding emerging leaders from around the world. Funded by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO), it enables high-achieving individuals with strong leadership potential to pursue a one-year master's degree at any UK university.

Chevening is not only focused on academic study but also on developing future global influencers, decision-makers, and professionals who can bring positive change in their home countries. Scholars are selected based on leadership qualities, academic background, and a clear vision for how they will contribute to solving real-world challenges after completing their studies in the UK.

Beyond financial support, the programme also offers access to a powerful global alumni network, professional development opportunities, and cultural exchange experiences in the UK. Since its creation in 1983, Chevening has built a strong international community of scholars working across government, business, science, and civil society worldwide.

Why Choose Chevening Scholarship?


Choosing the Chevening Scholarship is about more than just funding a master's degree—it is about stepping into a global leadership pathway. As a fully funded UK government scholarship, Chevening removes the financial barriers of studying abroad, allowing students to focus entirely on their academic and professional growth during a one-year master's program in the United Kingdom.

What makes Chevening especially valuable is its focus on leadership and impact. It is designed for individuals who have the potential to become future decision-makers in their countries, and it places strong emphasis on personal vision, experience, and ambition. Beyond the classroom, scholars become part of a powerful global alumni network that connects professionals across government, business, and civil society, creating lifelong opportunities for collaboration, influence, and career development.

Who Can Apply for Chevening Scholarship?

The Chevening Scholarship is open to outstanding individuals from around the world who have the ambition, leadership potential, and vision to pursue a one-year master's degree in the United Kingdom. It is designed for future leaders, influencers, and professionals who want to develop their skills, expand their global exposure, and bring positive change to their home countries.

In general, anyone who is a citizen of a Chevening-eligible country or territory can apply (CLICK HERE To See Eligible Countries), regardless of their background, age, gender, or personal circumstances. Applicants may be professionals from any field, including government, business, civil society, academia, or other sectors, as long as they demonstrate strong leadership experience and a clear purpose for studying in the UK.

Chevening is also open to individuals who have already completed their undergraduate studies and are looking to pursue postgraduate education. Applicants typically come from diverse career paths and are selected based on their potential to contribute meaningfully to their country after completing their studies in the UK.

Eligibility Criteria for Chevening Scholarship

To be eligible for the Chevening Scholarship, applicants must meet a set of strict requirements designed to identify future global leaders with strong academic, professional, and personal potential.

Citizenship Requirement
  • Must be a citizen of a Chevening-eligible country or territory (CLICK HERE To See Eligible Countries)
  • Applications are only accepted from countries included in the official Chevening list

Return Requirement
  • Must commit to returning to your home country for at least two years after completing the scholarship
  • This ensures scholars contribute to development in their own countries

Work Experience Requirement
  • Must have at least 2 years of work experience (2,800 hours)
    Experience can include:
  • Full-time employment
  • Part-time employment
  • Voluntary work
  • Paid or unpaid internships
  • Work experience is only counted after completing your undergraduate degree

Academic Requirement
  • Must hold an undergraduate degree that qualifies you for a UK master's programme
  • Degree must allow entry into a postgraduate course in the UK
  • University Application Requirement
  • Must apply to three different eligible UK master's courses
  • Must receive at least one unconditional offer from a UK university by the required deadline

Additional Requirements
  • Must meet the English language requirements of the chosen UK university (varies by institution)
  • Must submit required documents such as transcripts, degree certificates, and references
  • Must complete all application essays and application form sections fully

Ineligibility Conditions
Applicants cannot apply if they:
  • Hold British or dual British citizenship (with limited exceptions)
  • Have previously studied in the UK on a UK government-funded scholarship
  • Are employees or relatives of UK government, British Council, or partner organisations (with certain restrictions)
  • Hold refugee status in a country not eligible for Chevening

Benefits of Chevening Scholarship

The Chevening Scholarship is a fully funded UK government scholarship that provides extensive financial, academic, and professional support to international students studying a one-year master's degree in the United Kingdom.

Scholarship Benefits:
  • Full university tuition fees
  • Monthly living allowance (stipend)
  • Round-trip economy airfare to and from the United Kingdom
  • Arrival allowance
  • Homeward departure allowance
  • Visa application fee coverage
  • Travel grants for attending Chevening events across the UK
  • Access to world-class UK universities
  • Opportunity to study any eligible one-year master's program
  • Professional networking opportunities
  • Leadership development programs
  • Exclusive academic, cultural, and networking events
  • Access to the global Chevening alumni network
  • Career development and international exposure
  • Opportunity to connect with leaders, diplomats, professionals, and scholars from around the world

Additional Opportunities
Chevening Scholars also gain access to:
  • Conferences and seminars
  • Academic workshops
  • Networking receptions
  • Cultural activities across the UK
  • Professional development events organized by the UK Government and Chevening Secretariat
  • Global Alumni Network

One of the biggest benefits of Chevening is its international alumni network, which includes thousands of professionals, government officials, entrepreneurs, researchers, and leaders from more than 160 countries and territories. This network provides long-term professional connections and career opportunities even after graduation

Application Dates


  • Applications Open: 6 August 2026
  • Applications Close: 6 October 2026

Applications must be submitted through the official Chevening online application system before the closing date. Dates may vary slightly for future application cycles.

How to Apply for Chevening Scholarship

Applications for the Chevening Scholarship are submitted through the official Chevening Apply page, where you select your country or territory and access the scholarship portal for your location.
