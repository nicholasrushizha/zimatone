Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship 2027 for International Students 50%-Funded


Introduction

The Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship is a merit-based award offered by University of Adelaide to recognize outstanding international students with exceptional academic achievements. The scholarship is designed to attract high-performing students from around the world who wish to pursue undergraduate or postgraduate coursework studies at the University of Adelaide in Australia.

This scholarship rewards students who have demonstrated excellent academic results in their previous studies and provides a significant reduction in tuition fees. By supporting talented students from diverse backgrounds, the University of Adelaide aims to create an international learning environment that promotes academic excellence, innovation, and global engagement.

For students seeking a high-quality education in Australia, the Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship offers both financial support and recognition of academic achievement, making it an attractive opportunity for ambitious students looking to study at one of Australia's leading universities.

Why Choose Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship?

The Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship is an excellent opportunity for high-achieving international students who want to study in Australia while reducing the financial burden of university tuition. The scholarship recognizes students who have achieved exceptional academic results and rewards their hard work by providing a substantial tuition fee reduction throughout their studies.

One of the biggest advantages of this scholarship is that it is awarded based on academic merit, allowing outstanding students to gain recognition for their achievements before they begin their studies. This support can make a quality Australian education more accessible while enabling students to focus on their academic and personal development.

In addition to the financial benefits, receiving the Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship demonstrates a student's strong academic ability and commitment to excellence. It provides the opportunity to study at a respected Australian university, gain international experience, and build valuable skills and connections that can support future career success.

Who Can Apply for Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship?

The Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship is available to international students who plan to begin an eligible undergraduate or postgraduate coursework degree at Adelaide University. It is intended for students with exceptional academic achievements and provides a significant reduction in tuition fees.

To be considered, applicants must meet the university's high academic standards. Undergraduate applicants generally need results equivalent to an ATAR of 99, while postgraduate applicants must demonstrate outstanding academic performance in their previous studies. The scholarship is awarded on a competitive, merit-based basis.

The scholarship is not available for certain excluded programs, particularly some medicine and health-related degrees. It is also not open to Australia Awards recipients or students who already receive a tuition fee sponsorship that provides benefits equal to or greater than the scholarship value. Applicants must meet all admission requirements for their chosen program before they can be considered.

Eligibility Criteria for Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship

  • You must be an international student.
  • You must be applying for an eligible CRICOS-registered coursework degree at Adelaide University: either a bachelor's degree of at least 96 units after any credit/RPL, or a master's degree of at least 72 units after any credit/RPL.
  • You must meet the academic standard used for admission into the degree. For undergraduate applicants, this means a minimum ATAR of 99 or an international equivalent. For undergraduate and postgraduate applicants, this means a minimum overall GPA of 6.7 on a 7.0 scale or a comparable score.
  • You must not be applying for one of the excluded programs listed by the university. CLICK HERE to see excluded programs
  • You must not be an Australia Awards recipient.
  • You must not be an international sponsored student whose tuition fee waiver is equal to or greater than the scholarship value. The university says you cannot receive both the scholarship and a sponsor tuition waiver at the same time.

Benefits of Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship

  • 50% reduction in international tuition fees for each applicable study period. This is the main financial benefit of the scholarship.
  • Covers the standard full-time duration of the eligible undergraduate or postgraduate coursework program, as long as the student continues to meet the scholarship conditions.
  • Applied directly to the tuition fee account, so the scholarship reduces the amount the student has to pay for tuition rather than being paid out as cash.
  • Recognizes academic excellence, which adds strong value to a student's profile because it is a prestigious and highly competitive award for high-achieving international students.
  • Can be retained during an approved leave of absence of up to one year in exceptional circumstances, which gives some flexibility if study is temporarily interrupted.


Important note
The scholarship does not cover visas, OSHC, travel, accommodation, living expenses, textbooks, study materials, or other non-tuition costs. It is only a tuition fee waiver.

Scholarship Conditions Students Must Continue to Meet
To keep receiving the Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship throughout their studies, recipients must:
  • Remain an international student for the entire duration of the scholarship.
  • Maintain a minimum Program Term GPA of 5.5 out of 7.0 in every compulsory study period.
  • Continue making satisfactory academic progress in their enrolled program.
  • Remain enrolled in an eligible degree program covered by the scholarship.
  • Comply with all university rules, regulations, and scholarship requirements.
  • Maintain full-time enrolment for the standard duration of the program, unless approved otherwise by the university.


    • What Happens If the GPA Requirement Is Not Met?
  • If your Program Term GPA falls below 5.5/7.0, the scholarship may be suspended.
  • During the suspension period, the scholarship will not be applied to your tuition fees.
  • If your GPA later returns to 5.5/7.0 or higher, the scholarship can be reinstated and applied to future tuition invoices.

Application Dates

Semester 1: applications open on 24 August 2026.

Closing: Varies by program and intake.

How to Apply for Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship

The Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship requires a separate scholarship application in addition to an application for admission to an eligible program at Adelaide University. You must first apply for admission to an eligible undergraduate or postgraduate coursework program and obtain a valid application ID or Offer of Admission before you can apply for the Adelaide Academic Excellence Scholarship
