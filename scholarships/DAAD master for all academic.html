DAAD Study Scholarships - Master Studies for All Academic Disciplines 2027 for International Students Partly-Funded


Introduction

The DAAD Study Scholarships - Master Studies for All Academic Disciplines is a prestigious scholarship program funded by the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD). It is designed to support highly qualified international graduates who wish to pursue a master's degree at a state-recognized university in Germany. The scholarship aims to help talented students gain advanced academic training while benefiting from Germany's strong higher education system and research environment.

One of the key features of this scholarship is its broad academic scope. Unlike many scholarships that focus on specific fields, this program is open to applicants from almost all academic disciplines, allowing students from diverse educational backgrounds to pursue master's studies that align with their career goals and academic interests. This flexibility has made it one of the most popular international scholarship opportunities offered by Germany.

The scholarship is intended not only to support academic excellence but also to promote international cooperation and cultural exchange. Through studying in Germany, recipients have the opportunity to develop professional expertise, build international networks, and gain valuable experience in a globally respected education system. As a result, the program attracts outstanding graduates from around the world who are seeking high-quality master's education and international exposure.

Why Choose DAAD Study Scholarships?

This scholarship is a strong choice because it supports graduates who want to continue their academic training in Germany through a postgraduate or master's degree at a state or state-recognized university. It is funded by the German Federal Foreign Office and is designed for students with a first academic degree who want to study in Germany at a high level.

Another major advantage is its flexibility. The programme is open to almost all academic disciplines, so it gives students from many different fields the chance to study in Germany without being limited to only one subject area. DAAD also offers scholarships across a wide range of study and research opportunities, which makes the scholarship especially attractive for ambitious international graduates.

It is also valuable because the funding is tied to a full academic experience in Germany, with support lasting according to the normal duration of the master's programme. For many applicants, this means access to advanced study, international exposure, and a chance to build academic and professional connections in one of Europe's strongest higher education systems.

Who Can Apply for This Scholarship?

The DAAD Study Scholarships - Master Studies for All Academic Disciplines is open to graduates who have already completed their first university degree, such as a bachelor's degree or Diplom, by the time the funding period begins. It is meant for students who want to continue their studies with a postgraduate or master's programme at a state or state-recognized university in Germany.

This scholarship is suitable for applicants from almost all academic fields, which is one reason it is so popular. However, students in artistic disciplines and architecture, including Urban Design, are directed to separate DAAD scholarship programmes for those fields instead of this one.

Applicants should also be aware that the application cannot be considered if they have been living in Germany for longer than 15 months by the application deadline. In short, this scholarship is aimed at qualified graduates who are ready to pursue a full master's degree in Germany and who meet the programme's academic and residency rules.

Eligibility Criteria for This Scholarship

  • You must have completed your first university degree by the time the funding period begins. The DAAD notes that the scholarship is for graduates who want to continue their academic training in Germany with a postgraduate or master's course of study.
  • You must meet the country-specific conditions in the current call for applications, because DAAD says its general rules apply only when the scholarship announcement does not say otherwise.
  • Your eligibility can depend on your country of origin, country of residence, subject area, and language requirements, so the exact rules are not the same for every applicant.
  • You must apply through the current scholarship database listing for your programme, because DAAD says applicants should read the call for applications in the scholarship database and follow the application deadline and portal instructions there.
  • If you want to check whether your school certificates are sufficient for study in Germany, use the DAAD admission database, which lets you run a nonbinding check and currently covers around 140 countries.

Benefits of This Scholarship

What the scholarship covers
  • A monthly scholarship payment of €992.
  • Health, accident, and personal liability insurance support.
  • Travel allowance, but only if applied for.
  • Annual study allowance of €460.
  • Possible extra support after funding starts, including a monthly rent subsidy, family allowance, disability/chronic illness support, and a Green Mobility Top Up.
  • Language support, such as online language course fees, a preparatory German course if needed, an allowance for a German course during funding, and reimbursement for TestDaF or DSH fees.
  • The scholarship does not have to be repaid.


What the scholarship does not cover
  • Tuition fees are not covered by DAAD.
  • All living expenses are not automatically fully covered, so students may still need personal funds, especially in places with high rent.
  • Extra benefits are not guaranteed; rent subsidy, family allowance, and special-needs support are only available under certain conditions and must be approved.

How to Apply for This Scholarship

Applications for this scholarship are submitted through the official DAAD application system. Because application requirements, deadlines, and required documents may vary by country and scholarship call, applicants should carefully read the current scholarship announcement in the DAAD Scholarship Database before starting their application. The official scholarship listing contains the complete application procedure, document requirements, eligibility conditions, and submission instructions.
