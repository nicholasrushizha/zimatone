Türkiye Scholarships 2027 for International Students Fully-Funded

Introduction

The Türkiye Scholarships (known natively as Türkiye Burslari) is a highly competitive, government-funded international scholarship program designed for outstanding students and researchers from across the globe. Administered by the Presidency for Turks Abroad and Related Communities (YTB), it stands out globally because it offers a fully funded award that combines direct financial coverage with mandatory university placement, allowing recipients to secure both their funding and their academic seat simultaneously. The program aims to foster international cooperation, bridge cultures, and build a network of future global leaders across diverse fields including medicine, engineering, humanities, and natural sciences. Widely considered one of the most comprehensive packages in the world.

Furthermore, it grants a one-year Turkish language course to ensure smooth academic integration, alongside round-trip airfare from the student's home country. Open to undergraduate, master's, and PhD candidates, selection is based on stringent academic benchmarks.Ultimately, this prestigious initiative transforms Türkiye into a hub for international higher education, offering scholars a deeply enriched cultural experience and a launchpad for impactful, worldwide professional careers.

Why Choose Türkiye Scholarships?

Many international students choose Türkiye Scholarships because it offers more than just financial support. The scholarship gives students the opportunity to study at some of Türkiye's top universities while experiencing a rich culture that connects Europe and Asia. Since the program is fully funded, students can focus on their education without worrying about tuition fees, accommodation, health insurance, or flight costs.
Türkiye has become an attractive destination for higher education because of its modern universities, affordable living costs, and welcoming environment for international students. Through Türkiye Scholarships, students gain quality education, international exposure, cultural experience, and valuable career opportunities that can benefit them in the future.

Who Can Apply for Türkiye Scholarships?

Türkiye Scholarships is open to international students from almost every country in the world who want to study in Türkiye at undergraduate, master's, PhD, or research level. The scholarship is designed for academically successful students who show leadership potential and strong motivation for higher education.

Applicants must meet minimum academic requirements depending on the level of study.
There are also age limits for applicants. Undergraduate candidates must usually be under 21 years old, master's applicants under 30, PhD applicants under 35, and research scholarship applicants under 50 years old.

The scholarship accepts:
  • High school graduates
  • University graduates
  • Final-year students
  • Researchers and academics
However, Turkish citizens and students already studying in Türkiye at the same degree level they are applying are generally not eligible to apply. Türkiye Scholarships is highly competitive, so students with strong grades, leadership experience, volunteer activities, research background, and a well-written motivation letter usually have better chances of being selected.

Eligibility Criteria for Türkiye Scholarships

Türkiye Scholarships is open to international students from all countries who want to study in Türkiye at undergraduate, master's, PhD, or research level. Applicants must meet specific academic and age requirements to qualify.
  • For undergraduate programs, students must normally have at least 70% academic achievement
  • Master's and PhD applicants usually need a minimum of 75% academic performance,
  • while applicants for medicine, dentistry, and pharmacy programs must generally have at least 90%.
  • Undergraduate applicants must be under 21 years old.
  • Master's applicants should be under 30 years old,
  • and PhD applicants under 35.
  • Research scholarship candidates must usually be under 50 years old.
However, Turkish citizens and students already enrolled in Turkish universities at the same degree level are generally not eligible to apply.

The scholarship selection process is highly competitive, so students with strong academic records, leadership skills, volunteer activities, research experience, and well-prepared application documents usually have a better chance of being selected

Benefits of Türkiye Scholarships

Türkiye Scholarships provides international students with a complete fully funded study package in Türkiye. The scholarship benefits include:
  • Full tuition fee coverage
  • Monthly stipend for living expenses
  • Free university accommodation
  • Health insurance
  • Round-trip flight ticket
  • One-year Turkish language course
  • University and department placement
  • Access to top Turkish universities
  • Cultural and international networking opportunities
  • Opportunity to study in English or Turkish programs
These benefits allow students to focus on their education without worrying about major financial costs.

Application Dates

The application period for Türkiye Scholarships usually opens once every year between January and February. For the 2027 intake, applications open on 10 January 2027 and closed on 20 February 2026.
Important Timeline:
  • Applications Open: 10 January 2027
  • Applications Close: 20 February 2027
  • Evaluation Process: March - April (may be subject to change)
  • Interview Period: April - June (may be subject to change)
  • Results Announcement: Early August (may be subject to change)
  • Student Arrival in Türkiye: September 2027 (may be subject to change)
Students are encouraged to apply early to avoid technical issues and to have enough time to prepare all required documents.

How to Apply for Türkiye Scholarships

Official Scholarship Website:

Türkiye Scholarships Official Website(TBBS)

Applications for Türkiye Scholarships are submitted online through the official Türkiye Scholarships Application System (TBBS).

All application instructions, eligibility requirements, required documents, and application steps are provided on the official website. turkiyeburslari.gov.tr

Students can create an account, access the application portal, choose universities and programs, upload documents, and track their application status directly through the platform.
