CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program Scholarship 2027 for International Students Fully-Funded


Introduction

The CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program is a prestigious international scholarship funded by the Chinese Government and administered by the China Scholarship Council (CSC). The program aims to strengthen educational cooperation and cultural exchange between China and participating countries by providing financial support to international students who wish to pursue higher education in China.

Through this scholarship, students can apply for undergraduate, master's, doctoral, and non-degree study programs at selected Chinese universities. The scholarship is available to applicants from countries that have educational exchange agreements with China, making it one of the largest and most accessible government-funded scholarship opportunities for international students worldwide.

The CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program is highly valued because it offers comprehensive financial support and access to some of China's leading universities. By attracting talented students from around the world, the program promotes academic collaboration, cross-cultural understanding, and the development of future global leaders while helping recipients gain an internationally recognized education in China.

Why Choose CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program?

The CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program is a strong choice because it is designed to support a wide range of international students and scholars, including undergraduates, postgraduates, general scholars, and senior scholars. It is also backed by cooperation agreements between China and other governments or institutions, which makes it a recognized and well-structured government scholarship route.

Another major reason to choose it is the financial support. According to the official CSC guidance, the program can cover tuition fees, free university dormitory or an accommodation subsidy, a monthly stipend, and comprehensive medical insurance. The stipend amount is set by study level, with support for undergraduates, master's students, general scholars, doctoral students, and senior scholars.

It is also attractive because it supports both major study and Chinese-language preparatory study, and it offers access to designated Chinese universities across many fields such as science, engineering, medicine, economics, law, education, literature, and fine arts. Applicants go through the official dispatching or recommending agency in their home country, which keeps the process formal and clearly organized.

Who Can Apply for CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program?

The CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program is open to applicants who are citizens of countries other than the People's Republic of China and who are in good mental and physical health. It is designed for international students and scholars, and the official CSC guide says it supports undergraduates, postgraduates, general scholars, and senior scholars.

To qualify, applicants must meet the education and age requirements for the level they are applying for. This means having a high school diploma or equivalent for undergraduate study, a bachelor's degree for a master's program, a master's degree for a doctoral program, or the required academic background for scholar-level study, with age limits set by program type. For example, undergraduate applicants must be under 25, master's applicants under 35, doctoral applicants under 40, general scholars under 45, and senior scholars under 50.

Applicants must also meet the language requirements for their chosen program. For Chinese-taught programs, the CSC requires the relevant HSK level depending on the study level, and bachelor's applicants must also take the China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) and upload a valid score report. In addition, candidates for this bilateral program normally apply through the dispatching department or recommending authority in their home country, rather than applying only through a university.

Eligibility Criteria for CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program

  • You must be a citizen of a country other than the People's Republic of China and be in good mental and physical health.
  • For an undergraduate program, you must be an academically excellent holder of a high school diploma or its equivalent and be under 25 years old.
  • For a master's program, you must be an academically excellent holder of a bachelor's degree or its equivalent and be under 35 years old.
  • For a doctoral program, you must be an academically excellent holder of a master's degree or its equivalent and be under 40 years old.
  • For a general scholar program, you must hold at least a high school diploma and be under 45 years old.
  • For a senior scholar program, you must hold a master's degree or be an associate professor or above and be under 50 years old.
  • If you are applying for a bachelor's program, you must take the China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) and upload a valid score report.
  • If you are applying for a Chinese-taught general scholar program (except Chinese language majors), you must have passed HSK Level 3.
  • If you are applying for a Chinese-taught senior scholar program, you must have passed HSK Level 3.
  • If you are applying for a Chinese-taught master's or doctoral program, you must have passed HSK Level 4.
  • If you are applying for a program taught in another language besides Chinese, you must meet the university's own requirements for language ability, academic ability, and related admission conditions.

Benefits of CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program

  • Tuition fee coverage for the approved study program.
  • Free university dormitory or an accommodation subsidy if dorm housing is not provided.
    • Monthly living stipend:
  • Undergraduates: CNY 2,500
  • Master's students / general scholars: CNY 3,000
  • Doctoral students / senior scholars: CNY 3,500


  • Comprehensive medical insurance during the scholarship period. The official amount is CNY 800 per year for programs lasting more than six months, or CNY 400 for programs lasting less than six months.
  • Chinese language preparatory study support for one year when needed before the main degree or research program begins.
  • Access to designated Chinese universities and a wide range of majors, including science, engineering, medicine, economics, law, education, literature, philosophy, and fine arts.
  • Financial support for different study levels, including undergraduate, master's, doctoral, general scholar, and senior scholar categories.

Application Dates

Application dates vary by country, but the CSC Bilateral Program usually opens in November and closes between February and April of the next year.

How to Apply for CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program

The CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program is not applied for directly through the China Scholarship Council or a Chinese university. Applicants must submit their application through the official dispatching authority in their home country, which is usually the Ministry of Education, a government scholarship agency, or the Chinese Embassy/Consulate responsible for CSC nominations. Only candidates recommended by these authorities are considered for the scholarship.

After obtaining information from the dispatching authority, applicants must complete the online Chinese Government Scholarship application through the CSC system (Program Type A), prepare the required documents, and submit the complete application package according to the instructions provided by their country's dispatching authority. Some applicants may also obtain a pre-admission letter from a Chinese university if required or recommended.

CSC Scholarship-Bilateral Program Information Page

Applicants should visit the official CSC website and carefully read the latest application guide, required documents, procedures, agency number instructions, and country-specific requirements before starting their application. Exact application steps and deadlines may vary depending on the applicant's country and dispatching authority
