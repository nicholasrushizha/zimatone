Karen McKellin International Leader of Tomorrow Award (Canada) 2027 for International Students



Introduction

The University of British Columbia (UBC) offers several merit-based scholarships to recognize exceptional international students who demonstrate outstanding academic performance and active involvement beyond the classroom. Among the most notable of these awards are the International Major Entrance Scholarship (IMES) and the Outstanding International Student (OIS) Award, both designed to support talented students beginning their undergraduate studies at UBC.

These scholarships are awarded to international applicants who have distinguished themselves through academic excellence, leadership, community engagement, and extracurricular achievements. While the OIS Award is a one-time entrance scholarship, the IMES provides renewable funding for eligible students throughout their undergraduate studies, making both awards highly competitive opportunities for students seeking to study in Canada.

By offering financial support and recognizing exceptional achievement, these awards help attract some of the world's brightest students to UBC, enabling them to pursue a world-class education while continuing to develop their academic, leadership, and personal potential.

Why Choose This Scholarship?

The International Major Entrance Scholarship (IMES) and Outstanding International Student (OIS) Award are excellent opportunities for international students who have achieved strong academic results and demonstrated commitment beyond their studies. These awards recognize students who have not only excelled in the classroom but have also contributed through leadership, community service, extracurricular activities, or other meaningful achievements.

One of the main advantages of these scholarships is that they reward merit rather than financial need, allowing high-performing students to receive recognition for their accomplishments. The OIS Award provides valuable financial support during the first year of study, while the IMES offers the possibility of continued funding throughout an undergraduate degree, helping to reduce the overall cost of studying in Canada.

In addition to the financial benefits, receiving either of these awards is a significant achievement that highlights a student's academic excellence and potential. Being selected can strengthen a student's profile, provide greater opportunities for personal and professional growth, and allow them to pursue their education at one of Canada's leading universities with greater confidence and support.

Who Can Apply This Scholarship?

These awards are for new international students who are starting an undergraduate program at the University of British Columbia. They are meant for students entering UBC for the first time, especially those coming directly from secondary school or from post-secondary study outside UBC.

The scholarships are open to applicants who show strong academic performance and also have meaningful involvement outside the classroom, such as leadership, extracurricular activities, or community engagement. Since both awards are merit-based, they are aimed at students who have already shown excellence and promise before arriving at UBC.

The IMES and OIS awards are a good fit for international students who want to begin their studies at UBC and are competing for entrance scholarships based on achievement, not just financial need.

Eligibility Criteria This Scholarship

  • Must be an international student.
  • Must require a Canadian study permit to study at UBC.
  • Must be applying to UBC for the first time.
  • Must be entering an undergraduate degree program.
  • Must be entering directly from secondary school or another recognized post-secondary institution.
  • Must demonstrate outstanding academic achievement.
  • Must show strong involvement in extracurricular activities, leadership, community service, athletics, arts, or other significant pursuits outside the classroom.
  • Must meet all admission requirements for the chosen UBC undergraduate program.
  • Must submit an application for admission to UBC before the university's admission deadline.
  • Must not be nominated for UBC's need-and-merit-based International Scholars program.
    • Additional Requirement for IMES
  • Must maintain strong academic standing and satisfactory progress at UBC to remain eligible for scholarship renewal in future years.

Benefits of This Scholarship



International Major Entrance Scholarship (IMES)
  • Scholarship value: CAD $10,000 - $25,000 per year
  • Awarded to outstanding international students entering UBC undergraduate programs
  • Renewable for up to three additional years of study
  • Renewal depends on maintaining strong academic performance and meeting university requirements
  • Total funding received over the course of a degree may vary depending on the annual award amount and renewal status


Outstanding International Student (OIS) Award
  • Scholarship value: CAD $10,000 - $25,000
  • Awarded as a one-time entrance scholarship
  • Provided during the student's first year at UBC
  • Not renewable in subsequent years


Funding Summary
  • Both awards are merit-based scholarships that recognize academic excellence and involvement beyond the classroom.
  • Scholarship amounts may vary from year to year based on available funding and the strength of the applicant pool.
  • Funds can be used to help cover educational expenses such as tuition, books, accommodation, and other study-related costs.

Application Dates

There is no separate application deadline for IMES and OIS; applicants must apply to UBC by the admissions deadline to be considered.

How to Apply for This Scholarship

There is no separate scholarship application for the IMES or OIS awards. Students are automatically considered when they apply for admission to an eligible undergraduate program at the University of British Columbia. Applicants should submit their admission application through the official UBC admissions portal before the university's application deadline.
